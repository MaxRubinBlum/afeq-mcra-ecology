# Current descriptive findings (working, not final manuscript results)

The current mcrA analysis indicates strong spatial structure across sites and depth profiles.

- Methanomicrobiales, Methanosarcinales, and Methanobacteriales account for most classified mcrA reads.
- `Methanoregula` and `Methanosarcina` are major dominant lineages.
- EA2 is consistently `Methanoregula` rich.
- EA3 is strongly `Methanosarcina` dominated.
- EA1 contains the strongest recurrent ANME-2d / Methanoperedenaceae-related signal among the repeatedly sampled EA sites.
- At EA1, ANME-2d appears as a narrow recurring depth horizon rather than a simple monotonic increase with sediment depth.
- Additional ANME-2d enrichment occurs at P sites, especially P1 in April in the current exploratory summary.

These are **descriptive working results**. They should be regenerated from the ecology scripts after final metadata reconciliation and followed by appropriate inferential statistics.
